Why DBAs replay workloads

🎯

Capture the real thing

Top queries by total time from pg_stat_statements or dm_exec_query_stats — with their production timings as the baseline.

🔁

Replay with concurrency

Fire the workload at staging with configurable iterations and parallelism. Safe mode blocks writes unless you allow them.

📊

Before / after report

Per-query delta vs baseline, flagged slower/failed/blocked. Know exactly which queries your change hurt — before users do.

How it works

  1. 1

    Connect two databases: production (read the workload) and staging (replay target). Passwords are encrypted.

  2. 2

    Capture the top-N queries by total runtime. They become a named, reusable workload.

  3. 3

    Replay with N iterations × M concurrent workers. Each run is timed; errors are captured with the database's message.

  4. 4

    Compare. The report shows baseline vs replay per query — green when fine, orange when ≥50% slower, red when broken.
